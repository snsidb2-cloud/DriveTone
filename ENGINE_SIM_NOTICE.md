# Engine audio notice

DriveTone Lab v1.4 loads the open-source **Engine_Sim** browser engine-audio library at runtime.

Source: https://github.com/YigitSalihEmecen/Engine_Sim
Pinned revision: `f16e3f5ded382716f4342843d7e4463ebb7d0bd8`
Upstream license: MIT.

The library is not bundled into this ZIP; the prototype loads it from jsDelivr on first audio start. The named vehicle profiles in DriveTone are independent procedural tunings based on engine architecture/specifications. They do not contain official manufacturer recordings, logos, or manufacturer-owned sound files.
