# DriveTone Lab v1.4 — audio sources and naming

## Active engine audio
The active combustion sounds are generated in real time by the open-source **Engine_Sim** browser engine-audio library.

- Source: https://github.com/YigitSalihEmecen/Engine_Sim
- Pinned revision: `f16e3f5ded382716f4342843d7e4463ebb7d0bd8`
- Upstream license: MIT

No manufacturer recordings, OpenGameArt RPM loops, logos, or proprietary vehicle audio files are bundled or used by the active sound engine.

## Model profiles
The AMG GT 63, McLaren 720S, BMW M3/M4 Competition, Ferrari 458 Italia, Ferrari V12, and Porsche 911 GT3 labels identify independently tuned simulation profiles. The profiles use public vehicle/engine specifications as engineering references, then tune procedural exhaust/intake/turbo parameters artistically. They are not official manufacturer sound assets or exact acoustic replicas.
